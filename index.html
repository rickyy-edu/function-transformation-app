HKDSE S5 Function Transformation Notes

Universal Transformation Formula

$y = a \cdot f( b(x - h) ) + k$

  • Vertical Operations ($a, k$): Affect $y$-coordinates directly. Order: Stretch/Reflect ($a$), then Translate ($k$).
  • Horizontal Operations ($b, h$): Affect $x$-coordinates inversely. Inside $b(x - h)$, operation order on $x$: Translate right by $h$, then divide by $b$.
Constant '$a$' (Vertical Scaling)

• $|a| > 1$: Stretch vertically by factor $|a|$

• $0 < |a| < 1$: Compress vertically by factor $|a|$

• $a < 0$: Reflect in $x$-axis

Constant '$b$' (Horizontal Scaling)

• $|b| > 1$: Compress horizontally by factor $\frac{1}{|b|}$

• $0 < |b| < 1$: Stretch horizontally by factor $\frac{1}{|b|}$

• $b < 0$: Reflect in $y$-axis

Constant '$h$' (Horizontal Shift)

• $h > 0$: Shift Right by $h$ units

• $h < 0$: Shift Left by $|h|$ units

Constant '$k$' (Vertical Shift)

• $k > 0$: Shift Up by $k$ units

• $k < 0$: Shift Down by $|k|$ units
